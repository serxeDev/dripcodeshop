Our Story

Hey Graalians! I'm a pixel artist and longtime GraalOnline Classic player curating clean, ready-to-use shield and sword GFX codes so your character always stands out in Battle Arena and Onnet.

Drippy CodeShop started as a personal notebook of shield codes I kept losing track of between sessions. Every time I found a clean sprite I wanted to share it with the community, and every time the conversation turned into "does anyone have the code for this one?", it became obvious the archive needed a home.

What began as a handful of personal bookmarks slowly grew into a proper vault. Today it holds a curated set of shields and blades, each one previewed, checked and ready to paste straight into your profile status. No sign-up, no paywall, no clutter — just the code you need and a one-click copy button.

What We Do

Curated, not scraped

Every piece is drawn, reviewed and formatted by hand. We never bulk-mirror other people's work, and every artist keeps the credit for their own art.

One click to equip

Each card has a copy button that grabs the exact setshield or setsword code. Paste it into your profile status and the graphic is on your character.

Community first

Requests from the Discord shape the drop list. If the community wants a shield in a certain style, that is usually the next thing we draw.

Free and open

The archive is free, has no accounts, collects almost no data and works on any device. If you can play Graal, you can use the vault.

Getting Started

New here? It takes about twenty seconds to get your first shield on your character.

  1. Open the archive and pick a shield or sword you like.
  2. Hit the copy button on the card (or the code inside the preview).
  3. Open GraalOnline Classic, tap your character and paste the code into your Profile Status.
  4. Save your status and the graphic is equipped. That is the whole trick.

Need more detail? The codes archive opens with a built-in guide modal that walks through the same steps.
