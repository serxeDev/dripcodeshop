1. Overview

Drippy CodeShop is a small, fan-made archive for GraalOnline Classic artwork. We built it to share pixel art with the community, and we designed it so that it asks almost nothing of you in return. This policy explains what happens to the little data that is involved when you visit.

In short: we do not ask for your name, your email address or your GraalOnline account, we do not run advertising trackers, and we never sell your information. The only things stored are two small preferences saved in your own browser.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect information that identifies you personally. The archive is built out of static files, so there is no account system and no user database behind it.

The following is the complete list of what is stored, and it all lives on your own device:

  • Language preference — stored as drippy-lang, so the site remembers whether you read it in English or Spanish.
  • Page size preference — stored as drippy-page-size, so the archive keeps showing 15, 30, 45 or all entries the way you left it.

Both values are stored in your browser's local storage. They are never transmitted to us or to anybody else, and clearing your browser data removes them instantly.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

The Site sets no cookies. It uses local storage instead, which stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Unlike a cookie, it is never shared with the hosting provider or with any advertising network.

Because we store nothing in cookies, you never have to dismiss a cookie banner, and there is no tracking between the pages of this Site or between this Site and any other site.

4. How We Use Your Data

The two preferences we keep are used for one purpose only: to make the archive behave the way you left it. Your language choice decides whether the pages load in English or Spanish, and your page size choice decides how many cards appear per page.

We do not use them for profiling, advertising, analytics or any automated decision-making. Because the values never leave your browser, there is nothing for us to analyse in the first place.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information about you. There is nothing to share, because the Site never receives your personal information in the first place.

If you reach out to us on Discord or through social media, that conversation is governed by the privacy rules of the platform you use there. We will never add your contact details to a mailing list, and we will never message you outside the platforms you contacted us on unless you ask us to.

6. Third-Party Services

The Site loads the Pixelify Sans and Space Grotesk fonts from Google Fonts, and it loads the Tailwind CSS framework from a public CDN. Your browser therefore contacts those providers directly, which means Google may receive the usual technical request information such as your IP address and browser version.

We also link out to GraalOnline Classic, TikTok, Discord and the artist profiles on our Brands and Guild pages. Those are independent services with their own privacy policies, and we have no control over what they do with your data once you follow a link.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access it, to correct it, to have it deleted, to object to how it is used, or to take your data to another provider.

In practice, exercising those rights is easy: everything we store is already under your control, on your device. You can view or change your preferences at any time by switching the language button or the "Show Entries" dropdown, and you can erase everything by clearing site data in your browser settings. If you would like us to remove anything else, contact us and we will do it.

9. Children's Privacy

GraalOnline Classic is played by a broad audience, and some of it is young. The Site does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and because it collects no personal information from anyone at all, there is nothing specific to a child that we could hold onto.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information with us through Discord or one of our social channels, please contact us and we will remove it right away.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy, or a request to delete something, are always welcome. Reach us on Discord (the handle in the footer), or through the channels linked on our About page.
